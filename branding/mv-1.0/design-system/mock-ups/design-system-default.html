Default — v1.0, for Microverse

Small parts,
one working whole.

The base Microverse theme. Blue is the default because it's the color with the least baggage in software UI — trusted, legible, and unlikely to mean anything other than "this is a working product." Every other theme in the family (Vienna, Uhuru) starts here and swaps the palette; the components, spacing, and structure stay identical.

01 — Foundations

Color

Nine tokens. Blue carries most interface weight as the primary action color; sky and amber are both rationed to a single appearance per view — a live-status accent and a "new" accent, respectively.

01 — Foundations

Light & dark

Standard light/dark, no metaphor attached. The nine identity colors stay fixed; only background, border, and text tokens flip.

Light

Default surface

Near-white background, dark ink text, blue reading clearly as the primary action color.

Dark

Inverted surface

Near-black background, light text, blue and sky both brightened slightly to hold contrast.
