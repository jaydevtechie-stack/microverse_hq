Uhuru — v1.0, for Microverse

Small parts,
one umoja whole.

The palette traces to the colors most of the continent's flags share: red, gold, and green — carried through Ethiopia, the one nation that stayed uncolonized, and then picked up deliberately by newly independent states starting with Ghana in 1957. Ghana added one more, a black star, for African emancipation itself. Uhuru keeps all four, but still treats gold as the rarest of them — dawn, saved for the single moment freedom actually arrives.

01 — Foundations

Dawn & dusk

Freedom is often described as arriving — a first light, a morning after. Dusk mode isn't a negation of that, it's the hours before: near-black ground, the ember pulled brighter so it still reads as alive, and the dawn gold waiting at the edge of the palette for when it's needed again.
